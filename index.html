About Me

I am a PMP® certified Project Management professional and Certified ScrumMaster® with more than 14 years of experience delivering strategic initiatives, PMO governance, project controls, business transformation, and operational excellence across aviation, healthcare SaaS, logistics, and corporate services.

Throughout my career I have established governance frameworks, implemented project management best practices, improved reporting and executive visibility, managed risks and stakeholder expectations, and supported successful delivery of cross-functional programs. My approach combines structured governance with Agile delivery principles to ensure projects achieve business objectives while maintaining quality, transparency, and stakeholder alignment.

I enjoy solving complex business challenges, building efficient PMO processes, mentoring project teams, and using data-driven insights to support executive decision-making. My goal is to help organizations deliver strategic initiatives successfully while continuously improving project maturity and operational performance.
